Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

GroupChatGame ("the app," "we," "us") is a mobile app where private friend groups answer a daily question and see anonymous results. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we collect:

  • Account information: the email address and display name you sign up with. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never visible to us in plain text.
  • Group activity: the groups you create or join, group names, and your membership in them.
  • Answers you submit: your daily votes are stored so results can be calculated. Individual votes are never shown to other members, including group admins — only anonymous aggregate counts and percentages are ever displayed.
  • Push notification token: if you enable notifications, we store a device token (provided by Apple/Google via Expo's notification service) so we can send you a reminder when your group has a new question. If you deny or disable notifications, none of this is collected and the app works normally without it.

How we use this information

We use the information above only to operate the app: authenticating you, showing your groups and questions, calculating and displaying results, and sending the optional daily reminder notification. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

We rely on the following infrastructure providers to run the app. Each processes the minimum data needed to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and server-side functions.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device via Apple's and Google's own push notification services.

We do not currently use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated first.

Data retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Account → Delete Account inside the app. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, group memberships, votes, and push notification token. If you are the sole admin of a group, admin responsibilities are automatically handed to another member, or the group is removed if you were its only member. This action cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

GroupChatGame is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
